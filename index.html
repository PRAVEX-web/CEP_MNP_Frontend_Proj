MODULE 01📂

DigiLocker

DigiLocker is a Government of India digital platform for accessing and sharing digital documents.

Key things to know

  • It can provide access to issued digital documents.
  • Use the official DigiLocker website or app.
  • Keep account credentials and OTPs private.
  • Check document details before sharing them.
Safety tip: Never tell an OTP to someone claiming to be support staff.
MODULE 02🪪

Aadhaar Services

Official Aadhaar services help people access eligible services and manage selected Aadhaar-related tasks.

Key things to know

  • Use official UIDAI channels for Aadhaar services.
  • Review the website address before entering information.
  • Share Aadhaar information only when genuinely required.
  • Never publish Aadhaar numbers or OTPs in public.
Safety tip: This training website will never ask you to enter an Aadhaar number.
MODULE 03🧾

PAN Services

PAN-related online services include application, verification and other tax-service workflows.

Key things to know

  • Use official Income Tax Department or authorised service channels.
  • Understand the difference between official sites and lookalikes.
  • Do not send PAN details through unknown links or messages.
  • Be careful with unexpected fees or urgent requests.
Safety tip: Never enter your PAN details into this academic training demo.
MODULE 04🔐

Digital Safety

Good habits help you use online government services with more confidence.

  • ✓ Never share OTPs or passwords.
  • ✓ Verify the domain before signing in.
  • ✓ Avoid unknown links in SMS or messaging apps.
  • ✓ Use a screen lock and sign out on shared devices.
  • ✓ Share only information that is genuinely required.
Remember: If a message pressures you to act immediately, pause and verify it independently.
